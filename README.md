## Geo Spatial Map
#### How to use it
- Map: Displaus Zip code and Postal regions of Montgomery County of Maryland. 
- Dropbox: Allows users to filter regions by different accident severity to create a choropleth map.
- Checkbox: Option to toggle the visibility of accident locations on the map.
- Legend: Displays the color scale of choropleth map.
- Map Tooltips: Hover over any postal region details the counts of accident happend in that region.
- Location tooltips:Hover over any location icon details the Light, Collison Type, Vehicle make, Vehicle Model, Severity.

## Story:
Montgomery County, A small yet busy area in Maryland, is home to many diverse community of residents, commuters and bussiness interactions daily. Despite it's charm and vibrancy, this county faces a challenge that many urban and subran areas share i.e "Traffic accidents". These accidents may be minor fender bender to life threatning collions, which leaves an impactfull as well as overall safety of people in community.

To better understand this issue, we have created a dynamic choropleth map filled with color coded areas on map which represents the rate of injury sevrity from traffic accidents within that region. this map is not only about number, it's about people lives and story hidden with in the data. by simply hovering on zip code areas, we could learn number of accidents in that area broken down by the severity from "No apparent injury" to "Fatal injury". the map is also includes icons pinpointing exact locations, how, where and what type of vechiles involved in collions.

Through this, Montgomery County took a step closer to building a safer, more informed community, one where data isn’t just analyzed but put to work to make a tangible difference.
