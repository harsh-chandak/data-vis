# Mosaic Chart:

This project presents a comprehensive data visualization of traffic accidents using a **Mosaic Chart** to explore the relationship between light conditions, injury severity, and accident counts. It provides insights into patterns of traffic accidents to help improve road safety.

## Features

### 1. Mosaic Chart
- **Light Condition & Injury Severity**: Displays a distribution of accidents categorized by light conditions (e.g., Daylight, Dark - Lighted) and injury severity (e.g., No Injury, Serious Injury, Fatal Injury).
- **Color-Coded Legend**: A legend helps users quickly understand the mapping of colors to injury severity levels.

### 2. Interactive Elements
- **Tooltips**: Hover over the chart to see detailed information about light conditions, severity, accident count, and percentage.
- **Clickable Rectangles**: Click on any region in the chart to view detailed data.

### 3. Year Selection
- **Dropdown Filters**: Compare accident distributions for specific years or view all years combined.
- **Dual Year Comparison**: Allows simultaneous side-by-side comparisons of accident data for two different years.

### 4. Details View
- **Crash Details Table**: Displays filtered data such as crash date, time, vehicle make and model, weather, and surface conditions.
- **Time Distribution**: Visual representation of accident distribution across hours of the day.

## How to Use

### Interface
1. **Year Dropdown**: Choose a year from the dropdown menus at the top to filter accident data.
2. **Mosaic Chart**: Interact with the chart to explore accident distributions.
3. **Details Panel**: Click on any region in the chart to see a detailed breakdown of the selected category.

### Legend
- Understand the severity levels through the color-coded legend, located on the chart.

### Tooltip
- Hover over chart elements to view:
  - Light condition.
  - Severity level.
  - Accident count.
  - Percentage of total accidents.
